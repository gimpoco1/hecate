# Hecate

Hecate is a map of the world you have personally discovered.

Inspired by the torch-bearing Greek goddess of crossroads and thresholds, Hecate gradually reveals streets, neighborhoods, and cities as you move through them. Places you have visited emerge from the mist, while unexplored areas remain hidden and invite you to keep moving.

The app lets you move seamlessly between two views:

- **Discovered** shows the percentage of your current city that you have explored, based on its official municipal boundary.
- **Map** shows the complete map whenever you need a familiar view of the world.

You can begin from a globe, zoom into any city, follow your position as you travel, and watch the world appear smoothly around you. Your discoveries are stored privately and can follow you across your devices when you sign in.

Hecate is designed for everyday journeys, unfamiliar places, and the quiet satisfaction of seeing how much of the world has become yours.
