Getting started

  1. Create an account or sign in.
  2. Allow location access when Hecate asks for it.
  3. Tap the Start control to begin a walk.
  4. Tap Stop or Finish when you are done. Background location is used only during an active walk.

Location is unavailable

On your iPhone, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Hecate and allow location access. If you want a walk to continue while the phone is locked, allow the location access option requested by Hecate.

Signing in

If you use an email sign-in link, open it on the same device where you requested it. It should return you to Hecate. Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive.

Delete your account

Open the account and sync panel in Hecate, select Delete account, and confirm. This permanently removes your account, routes, and discoveries.

Contact support

Email supporthecate@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords or precise location history.
